# Privacy Policy for Iskra EV Companion

**Effective date:** November 19, 2026

Iskra EV Companion ("the App", package `com.iskra.companion`) reads live data from your electric vehicle through a Bluetooth OBD-II adapter and shows it on your phone and in Android Auto.

## Data collection

The App **does not collect, transmit, store off-device, or sell any personal data.**
- No account or sign-up.
- The App has **no internet access** (it does not request the INTERNET permission) and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
- Vehicle readings (battery, power, temperatures, tyre pressure and similar) are processed in memory on your phone and are never sent anywhere.

## Permissions

- **Bluetooth (Nearby devices):** to find and talk to your OBD-II adapter. On Android 11 and older, Android requires the location permission for Bluetooth discovery; the App does not read or store your location.
- No camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files access.

## Crash reports

If the App crashes, it shows a report on your screen with your device model, Android version and the error. The report stays on your phone. It is sent only if you choose to share it yourself.

## Local storage

Settings are kept on your device only and are removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Android backup is disabled for this App.

## Children

The App does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
